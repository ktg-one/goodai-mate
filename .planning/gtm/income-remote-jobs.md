## Practical notes (hypotheses)
- Fastest-moving routes are probably the agency role (#2) and the high-volume remote employers (#3, #4). Neither is guaranteed.
- Ask in the first call: earliest start date, pay frequency (weekly vs fortnightly), whether there is paid induction, and what ID or checks are needed. Have ID, a CV and a police-check route ready before applying **[H]**.
- Treat any role asking for payment, gift cards, or a purchase of equipment up front as a scam.
