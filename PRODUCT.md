# Product

## Platform
Web. Existing Next.js 16 App Router, React 19 and TypeScript stack retained.

## Audience and purpose
Business owners and operators dealing with repetitive administration, missed enquiries and disconnected tools. The supplied current company context prioritizes a credible Good’Ai commercial presence. This fork allows the founder to compare an alternate brand before choosing a public release.

## Product truth
Five public service categories from the supplied finalized list: AI voice agents, business automation, custom AI assistants, AI integration projects, and an AI opportunity audit. Support is an add-on. All intake links continue using lib/links.ts. Demonstrations are local examples except the opt-in existing ElevenLabs integration.

## Voice and evidence
Keep Good'Ai spelling, plainspoken Australian English and human control. Do not invent testimonials, performance figures, compliance claims or active clients. Historical brand decisions inform tone but do not constrain this authorized redesign.

Founder feedback on the alternate: keep its visual structure and content, and restore the original handwriting and GSAP storytelling. The emotional promise is “Take a break. Knock off early. We’ll sort the work.” Vibes asides and scroll-drawn illustrations now carry that reassurance, while service descriptions retain concrete scope and human review.

## Open decisions
Kevin selected the alternate branding on 13 September 2026. Production legal reconciliation, final commercial approval and real photography remain release tasks. The current fork is available locally.
